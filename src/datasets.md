## Employee Attrition Dataset Summary

### Number of Columns

This dataset contains **35 columns**.

### List of Columns and Their Purpose

- **Age**: Employee’s age
- **Attrition**: Whether the employee left the company (“Yes” or “No”)
- **BusinessTravel**: Frequency of business travel
- **DailyRate**: Employee’s daily salary rate
- **Department**: Department (e.g., Sales, R&D, HR)
- **DistanceFromHome**: Distance from home to work
- **Education**: Education level (numeric)
- **EducationField**: Field of education (Life Sciences, Medical, etc.)
- **EmployeeCount**: Number of employees (usually constant)
- **EmployeeNumber**: Unique employee identifier
- **EnvironmentSatisfaction**: Satisfaction with work environment (scale)
- **Gender**: Employee’s gender
- **HourlyRate**: Hourly pay rate
- **JobInvolvement**: Level of involvement in the job (scale)
- **JobLevel**: Job level (numeric)
- **JobRole**: Employee’s job title or role
- **JobSatisfaction**: Satisfaction with job (scale)
- **MaritalStatus**: Marital status
- **MonthlyIncome**: Monthly income
- **MonthlyRate**: Monthly pay rate
- **NumCompaniesWorked**: Number of companies worked at
- **Over18**: Is the employee over 18 years old (usually “Y”)
- **OverTime**: Does employee work overtime (“Yes” or “No”)
- **PercentSalaryHike**: Percentage increase in salary
- **PerformanceRating**: Performance rating (scale)
- **RelationshipSatisfaction**: Satisfaction with relationships at work (scale)
- **StandardHours**: Standard working hours (usually constant)
- **StockOptionLevel**: Employee’s stock option level
- **TotalWorkingYears**: Total years of professional experience
- **TrainingTimesLastYear**: Number of trainings attended last year
- **WorkLifeBalance**: Work-life balance rating (scale)
- **YearsAtCompany**: Years at current company
- **YearsInCurrentRole**: Years in current job role
- **YearsSinceLastPromotion**: Years since last promotion
- **YearsWithCurrManager**: Years working with current manager

### Purpose of the Dataset

This dataset is used to analyze employee attrition (turnover). It helps organizations to:

- Predict which employees are likely to leave
- Identify factors that contribute to employee departures
- Understand patterns and risks in various departments or demographics
- Develop strategies to improve retention and workplace satisfaction
