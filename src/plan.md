# Define the Problem and Plan

### Attrition on a Company

Employees are the heart on any company. How well a company does depends a lot on its people.
When employees leave, it creates several problems for the business:


- Training new hires costs a lot of money and takes up valuable time.
- The company loses experienced people who know the job well.
- Overall productivity can drop.
- Profits may be affected.


Before we start working with data, it's important to clearly define our business questions.
Knowing exactly what problem we're trying to solve will help us build a useful solution.
If the problem isn't clear, the solution won't make sense.

## Business Questions for Employee Attrition Analysis

Here are some important questions to consider  when analyzing employee attrition:

1. **What are the main reasons employees leave the company?**

2. **Which departments or teams have the highest turnover rates?**

3. **Are there any pattern in employee attrition based on age, experience, or job level?**

4. **How does employee attrition affect overall company productivity and profitability?**

5. **Can we predict which employees are most likely to leave soon?**
